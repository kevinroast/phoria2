# Sample assets

- `DamagedHelmet.glb` - "Battle Damaged Sci-fi Helmet - PBR" by theblueturtle_, glTF conversion
  by Khronos. CC BY 4.0. From https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets
- `SheenChair.glb` - "Sheen Chair", (c) 2020 Wayfair, LLC, by Eric Chadwick. CC0 1.0. From
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets
- `ABeautifulGame-instanced.glb` - "A Beautiful Game" (c) 2020 ASWF (MaterialX Project), glTF (c) 2022
  Ed Mackey. CC BY 4.0. From https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets - packed for phoria2 with
  gltfpack 1.3 (`-cc -mi -tc -tl 1024`): meshopt geometry, repeated pieces instanced
  (EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing), KTX2 textures at 1024 px.
- `GlassVaseFlowers.glb` - "Glass Vase with Flowers": vase by Eric Chadwick, flowers by Rico Cilliers.
  CC0 1.0. From https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets
- `CesiumMilkTruck.glb` - by Cesium (Analytical Graphics, Inc.). CC BY 4.0. From
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets
- `skull.glb` - the skull's 22 bones and 28 teeth from BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life
  Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International. From
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ - converted to one GLB, a node per bone and
  tooth, the jaw pivoted at its joint, ambient occlusion baked into its vertex colours.
- `../hdr/studio_small_09_1k.hdr` - "Studio Small 09" by Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven. CC0.
  From https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09
- `../video/mandelbrot.mp4` - a zoom into the Mandelbrot set's Seahorse Valley, rendered for phoria2
  with ffmpeg's `mandelbrot` source; no third-party material.
